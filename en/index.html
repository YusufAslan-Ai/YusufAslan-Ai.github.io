About

I'm Yusuf Aslan. I work on digital marketing and Meta advertising. I help businesses manage their ad budgets with data instead of gut feeling.

My approach is simple: first we open the account together and look at what works and what burns money. Then we scale the winner and shut down the loser. I report every step in plain, clear language.

The Right Order

First find the winner, then grow the budget.

01

Find the winner.

We review every campaign in your account one by one. We mark the ad set, creative and audience that carry your ROAS. The metric we look at is sales, not likes.

02

Scale it.

We scale the winner with Advantage+ and manual campaigns. We recreate the creative and expand the audience in a controlled way. A/B tests every week.

03

Shut down the loser.

We stop the ads that burn money and move the budget to the winner. The weekly report states which decision led to which result — clearly and simply.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What people ask before applying.

How much does Meta ads management cost?

There is no fixed package price; the fee depends on your needs, the number of campaigns and the scope of work. In the free first consultation we discuss what you need and I give you a clear, tailored price.

What is the minimum monthly ad budget?

It depends on the industry. An e-commerce store and a service business have different needs. We set the right budget for your sector and goals together in the first consultation.

Is there a contract or minimum term?

The working period and contract terms are agreed according to your needs once we decide to work together. What matters most to me is a partnership built on trust.

Who pays the ad budget?

You pay the ad budget directly to Meta with your own credit card. The budget always stays in your account and under your control; my fee covers the management service only.

How soon do results start to show?

If there are no gaps on the product or service page and in tracking, results usually start to show quickly. We check for those gaps together in the free first consultation.

Do you only work with businesses in Turkey?

No. I work with businesses in Turkey and abroad; location doesn't matter.

How are the trainings delivered?

Meta ads and Trendyol trainings are held online and live, so you can join from anywhere.

Is the first consultation paid?

No, the first consultation is free. We open your ad account together and talk about which ads are wasting money and which change will make an immediate difference.
