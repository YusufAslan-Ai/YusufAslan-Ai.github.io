Etsy listing SEO: titles, tags and photos

By Yusuf Aslan · 4 min read · October 2026

What does the buyer search for?

On Etsy, whether a listing is found depends on how well it matches the buyer’s search. When describing your product, think in the words the buyer will type, not your own.

Write the title naturally and clearly

Put what the product is, its material and its purpose in the title. Repeating the same word hurts readability. Let the first part of the title carry the most important information.

Do not waste tags

Use every available tag and choose phrases that cover different searches rather than repeating each other. Multi-word phrases usually carry clearer intent than single words.

Photos and the product story

Etsy buyers often look for handmade or special items. Photos that show scale and use, plus a short description of how it is made, build trust. State processing and shipping time clearly.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't my Etsy listing show up in search?

Being found on Etsy depends on your listing matching what the buyer types. Describe the product with the words a buyer would search for, not your own.

How should I write an Etsy title?

State what the product is, its material and what it's for in natural language, with the most important information first. Repeating the same word over and over hurts readability.

How should I choose Etsy tags?

Use every tag slot and pick non-repeating phrases that cover different searches. Longer multi-word tags usually signal clearer buying intent than single words.
