How to write content that AI will quote

By Yusuf Aslan · 5 min read · October 2026

Give the answer first

Ask the question in the heading and give a clear 1–2 sentence answer right below it. Expand on the details afterward.

Write definitions clearly

Sentences that make sense on their own, like "X is the thing that lets Y do Z...", are good for quoting.

Add a Q&A section

Collect the questions customers really ask and answer each under its own heading.

Dates and updates

Show the publish and update dates. Outdated information doesn't get quoted.

Be concrete

Instead of "very good", give measurements, durations, price ranges and step counts.

Show the author

Who wrote it, and what experience do they have? Expertise builds trust.
