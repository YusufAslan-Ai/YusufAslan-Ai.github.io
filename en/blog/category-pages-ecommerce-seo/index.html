Category pages are e-commerce's most underused SEO opportunity

By Yusuf Aslan · 4 min read · October 2026

Most e-commerce sites focus on product pages. But broad searches like "women's leather jacket" go to category pages. If a category page is only a product list, it has nothing to tell the search engine.

Original intro text

Write an 80–150 word description at the top that helps the buyer choose. It must be different for every category.

Title and description

Use the main search term in the page title and give a clickable benefit in the description.

Internal links

Link to subcategories and best sellers. This is how search engines learn which pages matter.

Filter pages

Don't let every filter combination be indexed. Hundreds of copies of the same content hurt rankings.

Frequently asked questions

Why do category pages matter for SEO?

Broad searches like "women's leather jacket" usually land on category pages, not product pages. A category page that is only a product grid gives the search engine nothing to understand.

How much text should I add to a category page?

A unique 80–150 word introduction at the top that helps the buyer choose is enough. The text should be different for every category.

Should filter pages be indexed?

Don't let every filter combination be indexed. Hundreds of copies of the same content hurt rankings.
