How to raise your Trendyol seller score

By Yusuf Aslan · 4 min read · October 2026

Why the score matters

On a marketplace the customer sees the product, not your store. But a low seller score pushes your listings down and makes it harder to join promotions. Protecting the score is the cheapest investment you can make before spending on ads.

Three areas that move the score

The exact metrics a platform uses can change, so always check the current rules in your seller panel. In practice three areas always matter:

  • Shipping speed: handing orders to the carrier within the time you promised.
  • Customer communication: answering questions and messages quickly and clearly.
  • Returns and complaints: the product that arrives must match the listing exactly.

How to lower the return rate

Most returns come from a gap between expectation and reality. A product page that states size, colour, material and box contents clearly, with real photos, cuts returns directly. To find problem products, simply look at the five items with the most returns.

Build a weekly routine

Check the same three numbers every week: share of late shipments, unanswered questions and return reasons. You catch problems while they are small. Over time a store that does this pulls ahead of a competitor selling the same product.
