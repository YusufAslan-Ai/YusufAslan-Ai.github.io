Conversion and marketplace integration in your Ticimax store

By Yusuf Aslan · 5 min read · October 2026

Selling on your own site is different from a marketplace

On a marketplace the customer arrives with trust in the platform. On your own site you build the trust. That is why clear information on the product page, trust signals and an easy checkout decide results in your own store.

First conversion checks

  • Are photos, size, shipping and return details on the first screen of the product page?
  • Does checkout force sign-up or have too many fields?
  • Have you placed an order from a phone yourself?

Care with marketplace integration

Keeping stock and price in sync with marketplaces reduces the risk of cancellations and negative ratings after a sale. In the first days after integration, check stock and price by hand.

Set up measurement

Before advertising, test that conversion tracking matches real orders. Otherwise Meta and Google reports do not reflect reality and budget goes to the wrong place.
