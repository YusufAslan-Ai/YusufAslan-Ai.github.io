Selling abroad on Trendyol: how micro export works

By Yusuf Aslan · 7 min read · November 2026

Trendyol micro export is how a seller based in Türkiye lists products on Trendyol’s international storefronts and ships foreign orders the same way as domestic ones. Trendyol starts the customs registration; the seller has to meet the eligibility criteria, choose the countries, enter the correct origin and HS (GTİP) codes and issue the export invoice. Eligibility depends on seller scores, recent orders and store age, and it is checked again every month. Below I explain who can join, how to switch it on step by step and which costs sellers tend to miss.

The topic is timely because the November campaigns showed how big foreign demand has become. According to Trendyol’s statement of 17 November 2026, sellers sold 77 million products in the first half of November, and 11 million of them went to customers abroad. Riyadh, Baku and Bucharest were the cities with the most foreign orders. If your store sells well in Türkiye, some of those products may already have buyers in other countries.

How does Trendyol micro export work?

Your product is listed on a Trendyol storefront abroad (Romania, Saudi Arabia or Azerbaijan, for example). A customer there orders it and you hand the parcel to the carrier as usual. The Turkish Exporters Assembly (TİM) published a Trendyol marketplace guide in April 2026. It says that once you select a country, Trendyol starts the customs registration automatically and the listing screens become active within a few working days.

The same guide lists 17 countries where Trendyol is active and notes that Romania and Saudi Arabia are the largest markets after Türkiye. Not every country opens to every seller on the same terms, though; each region has extra conditions. The definitive answer to “which countries can I sell to?” is the country selection screen in your own seller panel.

Who is eligible for micro export?

According to the TİM guide, Trendyol reviews eligibility every month. The main criteria are:

CriterionWhat it means
Invoice historyAt least one invoice uploaded to the system within the last year.
ScoresSeller score, violation score, return and supply scores above the threshold.
Store age and activityAt least 60 days since the first product upload and at least one order in the last 30 days.
CategoryThe product category must be legally and operationally suitable for export.
Region-specific rulesEurope: at least 30 export-eligible products in the region. Gulf and Azerbaijan: at least 40 orders in the last 180 days.

The scores row matters most. The guide does not publish the thresholds, but the logic is clear: Trendyol will not risk a foreign customer on a store that ships late or gets many returns at home. If your score is borderline, work on raising your Trendyol seller score first; export comes after that.

How do you start selling abroad on Trendyol, step by step?

  1. Check eligibility. Eligible sellers see a “Mikro İhracat” button on the panel home page. If it is missing, you do not qualify yet; review your scores and order history.
  2. Choose countries. Tick the countries on the selection screen and save. According to the TİM guide, Azerbaijan and Europe also require a contract approval; the Gulf region does not.
  3. Fix origin and HS codes. The guide is explicit: checking and updating origin and HS (GTİP) codes is the seller’s responsibility. Products with Türkiye as origin automatically get a “Made in Türkiye” label on the foreign product page. A wrong HS code means customs trouble, so do this with your accountant.
  4. Open products for export. Under Products > Export Products you can open items per country one by one, in bulk or with an automatic rule. Start with best sellers that have low return rates instead of opening everything.
  5. Set up invoicing. Export invoices differ from domestic ones. Birfatura’s guide, updated in November 2026, says these sales use an e-Archive exemption invoice and the shipping label cannot be printed before the invoice is uploaded. Make sure your accounting software supports this invoice type.
  6. Ship and track. Hand over the parcel as you would a domestic order. In the first weeks, note delivery times and return reasons for foreign orders separately; that data shows which countries suit you.

What should you watch in the profit calculation?

The most common mistake I see is assuming a product that is profitable in Türkiye will automatically be profitable abroad. Birfatura’s guide says export listings do not keep a separate price and stock; your Türkiye listing is the base. So every discount you run at home may also apply to foreign sales. Check in your panel how your price appears abroad.

The costs to include are the category commission, any service fee specific to international sales, shipping, the cost of foreign returns and the payment term. These change from period to period, so take them from the current contract and tariffs in your seller panel rather than from a list on another website. As the TİM guide explains, you can download commission and advertising invoices filtered by country under the Finance menu; that is the easiest way to track export profitability on its own.

How should product pages be prepared for foreign buyers?

A customer in Riyadh or Bucharest cannot touch your product; they understand size and material from your page. Returns cost more abroad, so the gap between expectation and reality has to be close to zero. A size chart, true-colour photos and a clear list of what is in the box are essential; I cover how I prepare them in marketplace product images and descriptions. A title that clearly states brand, product type and key feature also helps your product match foreign searches; see how to write a marketplace product title.

Which other channels can you use to sell abroad?

The advantage of Trendyol micro export is that you start with your existing store and the same operations. It is not the only channel, though. Frequently asked questions

Do I need a separate company for Trendyol micro export?

No, you start with your existing Trendyol seller account. What matters is meeting the eligibility criteria and having invoicing software that can issue export invoices. Clarify tax and VAT refund questions for your company type with your accountant.

Why can’t I see the micro export button?

According to TİM’s Trendyol guide, the button only appears for sellers who meet the criteria, and eligibility is reviewed every month. Check your seller, violation, return and supply scores, whether 60 days have passed since your first product upload and whether you had an order in the last 30 days.

Who handles customs?

After you select a country, Trendyol starts the customs registration automatically and you ship the parcel like a domestic order. Making sure origin and HS (GTİP) codes are correct is the seller’s responsibility.

Can I set a different price abroad?

Birfatura’s current guide says export listings do not keep a separate price and stock; your Türkiye listing is the base. Expect domestic discounts to carry over to foreign sales and check in your panel how your price appears abroad.

Which countries can I sell to?

The TİM guide lists 17 countries where Trendyol is active, with Romania and Saudi Arabia as the largest markets after Türkiye. Which ones open to you depends on region-specific conditions; the definitive list is the country selection screen in your seller panel.
