Shopify SEO settings: 8 things to check first

By Yusuf Aslan · 6 min read · November 2026

Shopify SEO settings come down to a handful of fields that decide how your store looks in search results: the homepage and product titles, meta descriptions, image alt text, URL redirects and your Google Search Console connection. Shopify generates the sitemap, robots.txt and canonical tags for you; your job is to fill in the parts that can’t be automated. Work through the eight checks below in order and your store’s SEO foundation will be solid.

Many store owners spend weeks choosing a theme and apps, then leave the text search engines actually read blank or on autopilot. The platform changes, the logic doesn’t: I covered the same checks for a different admin panel in basic SEO settings in an Ideasoft store, and for ikas stores in my ikas SEO settings and redirects guide.

What does Shopify handle for SEO automatically?

Start by knowing what you don’t need to touch. According to Shopify’s own SEO overview:

  • Canonical tags are added automatically to keep duplicate content out of search results.
  • sitemap.xml and robots.txt are generated automatically.
  • Themes generate title tags that include your store name.
  • SSL certificates are on by default.
  • Themes include schema markup for products.

So most of the technical groundwork is done. But the “automatic” titles are usually just the product name plus the store name. They don’t tell a searcher why they should click. The real difference comes from the manual settings below.

How do you set up Shopify SEO in 8 steps?

  1. Turn off password protection. Search engines can’t see a password-protected store.
  2. Write the homepage title and meta description under Online Store > Preferences.
  3. Give every product and collection its own title and description in the search engine listing preview.
  4. Expand product descriptions with original copy instead of short supplier text.
  5. Add alt text to images that describes what the image shows.
  6. Set up redirects whenever you change a URL.
  7. Connect Google Search Console and submit your sitemap.
  8. Test your theme’s structured data to confirm price and stock show correctly.

Why isn’t my Shopify store showing up on Google?

The most common reason is that the store is still password protected. Shopify’s online store preferences page says password protection restricts access for search engines as well as visitors, and its sitemap guide notes that Google can’t read your sitemap while the store is in private mode. Removing the password should be the first thing you do on launch day.

The second reason is impatience. Shopify says changes can take a couple of weeks to show up in search results, so don’t treat a new store’s missing rankings in week one as a problem.

Where do you change the page title and meta description in Shopify?

For the homepage, use the “Social sharing image and SEO” section under Online Store > Preferences. For products, collections, pages and blog posts, open the item and edit the search engine listing preview at the bottom. Shopify’s guide to adding keywords says titles can be up to 70 characters but recommends 60 or fewer to avoid truncation, and about 160 characters for meta descriptions.

A concrete example: instead of “Leather Bag – StoreName”, write “Women’s Leather Shoulder Bag, Full-Grain | StoreName”. That puts both the search term and a differentiator in the title. In the description, give a reason to click, such as delivery time or easy returns. Google sometimes rewrites titles and descriptions, but well-written ones make that less likely.

Collection pages deserve extra attention, because broad searches like “women’s boots” often land on a collection rather than a product. Give each collection an original intro; I explain why in category pages and e-commerce SEO.

How long should a product description be?

Shopify recommends at least 250 words of descriptive text on every page and at least 500 words on informational pages and blog posts. Rather than counting words, ask whether you’ve answered what a buyer wants to know before purchasing: size, material, use, care, shipping and returns. Copying the supplier’s text means your page says exactly what dozens of other sites say. Write in your own words and add a short FAQ to the product page.

How do you add alt text to images?

Alt text helps search engines understand an image and makes your store accessible to people using screen readers. In Shopify you click a product image and choose to edit its alt text; for collection and blog featured images you edit it from the image settings. Replace “IMG_4821” with something like “brown full-grain leather women’s shoulder bag, side view”.

What happens if I change a URL in Shopify?

Visitors and search engines following the old link hit an error page, so you need a redirect. Shopify’s URL redirect guide notes:

  • You can only redirect from broken URLs; if the old URL still loads, the redirect won’t apply.
  • You can bulk import redirects from a CSV file.
  • A store can have up to 100,000 redirects.
  • Fixed paths such as /products or /collections can’t be redirected themselves.

If you’re migrating to Shopify from another platform, this step is critical: list every old product and category URL, map each to its new Shopify URL and import them on migration day.

How do you submit your sitemap to Search Console?

Shopify creates the sitemap at the root of your domain (for example yourstore.com/sitemap.xml). It links to separate sitemaps for products, collections, blogs and pages, and updates itself as you add content. Before you can submit it you need to verify your domain in Google Search Console; Shopify’s suggested method is pasting Google’s meta tag into your theme.liquid file just below the opening <head> tag. Then enter “sitemap.xml” in the Sitemaps section of Search Console.

Once it’s connected, check the Pages report once a month for products that aren’t indexed, old URLs returning 404 and duplicate pages. My 10-point SEO and GEO checklist turns that into a routine.

Is your theme’s structured data enough?

Most Shopify themes include product schema, but “included” doesn’t mean “correct”. Run a product page through Google’s Rich Results Test and confirm that price, currency and availability match what’s on the page, especially during sales when prices change. Frequently asked questions

Is Shopify good for SEO?

The technical base is solid: canonical tags, the sitemap, robots.txt and SSL are automatic, and themes include product schema. What really moves rankings is the titles, descriptions and product copy you write.

How long should a Shopify meta description be?

Shopify recommends about 160 characters. Page titles can be up to 70 characters, but 60 or fewer is recommended so they are not cut off in search results.

Where is my Shopify sitemap?

At the root of your domain, for example yourstore.com/sitemap.xml. It is created automatically and updates as you add content. Google cannot read it while the store is password protected.

Does Shopify create a redirect automatically when I change a product URL?

Don’t rely on it. After changing a URL, check URL redirects to confirm the old address points to the new one, and add it manually if not. Redirects only work from URLs that no longer load.
