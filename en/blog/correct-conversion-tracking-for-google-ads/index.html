Set up Google Ads conversion tracking correctly before you spend

By Yusuf Aslan · 5 min read · October 2026

No tracking means no optimisation

Google’s automated bidding learns by knowing which click became a sale. If conversion data is wrong or missing, the algorithm optimises the wrong thing and budget is wasted.

Choose what counts as a conversion

It should be a real business result, not a page view: a purchase, add to cart, form submission or phone call. If you mark everything as a conversion, numbers inflate and decisions get harder.

Pass the purchase value

Send the cart value, not just "a sale happened". That way Google sees which ad brings more revenue and can work toward a ROAS target.

Check for double counting

If the same sale is counted twice by Google Ads and Analytics, ROAS looks better than reality. After setup, place a test order and confirm it appears once; a few days later compare with your order system.

Frequently asked questions

Why is conversion tracking so important in Google Ads?

Google's automated bidding learns by knowing which clicks turn into sales. If conversion data is missing or wrong, the algorithm optimizes for the wrong thing and budget is wasted.

What should I count as a conversion in Google Ads?

A real business result, not a page view: a purchase, add to cart, form submission or phone call. If you mark everything as a conversion, the numbers get inflated.

How do I check that conversions aren't counted twice?

After setup, place a test order and confirm it appears only once in the dashboard. A few days later, compare the numbers with your own order system.
