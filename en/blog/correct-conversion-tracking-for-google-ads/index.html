Set up Google Ads conversion tracking correctly before you spend

By Yusuf Aslan · 5 min read · October 2026

No tracking means no optimisation

Google’s automated bidding learns by knowing which click became a sale. If conversion data is wrong or missing, the algorithm optimises the wrong thing and budget is wasted.

Choose what counts as a conversion

It should be a real business result, not a page view: a purchase, add to cart, form submission or phone call. If you mark everything as a conversion, numbers inflate and decisions get harder.

Pass the purchase value

Send the cart value, not just "a sale happened". That way Google sees which ad brings more revenue and can work toward a ROAS target.
