How to increase your e-commerce conversion rate

By Yusuf Aslan · 5 min read · October 2026

Find where you lose people first

Before adding visitors, understand why current visitors do not buy. Look at how many leave at the product page, cart and checkout steps. Start at the step with the biggest drop.

Build trust on the product page

  • Sharp photos and size information.
  • Real reviews.
  • Delivery time and return terms visible on the first screen.

Shorten checkout

Mandatory sign-up, too many fields and surprise shipping costs are the main causes of cart abandonment. Guest checkout and showing the total early make a difference in most stores.

Test on mobile

If most traffic comes from phones, place an order from your own phone. Slow pages, small buttons and a hard checkout silently eat your ad budget.

One change at a time

If you change many things at once you cannot tell what worked. Make one change, watch for a few weeks, and note the result.
