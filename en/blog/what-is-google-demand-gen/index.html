What is Google Demand Gen? A guide for e-commerce

By Yusuf Aslan · 8 min read · November 2026

Demand Gen is the Google Ads campaign type that runs image and video ads across YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Discover, Gmail and the Google Display Network. Where a Search campaign captures demand that already exists, Demand Gen creates interest among people who are not searching for you yet. For e-commerce it works best with a Merchant Center product feed, strong video and image creatives and properly set up conversion tracking. Below I explain how it works, how I set it up and how I plan the budget, based on Google’s own help pages.

What exactly is a Demand Gen campaign?

According to Google’s overview of Demand Gen campaigns, these ads run on YouTube (including Shorts, in-stream and in-feed), Discover, Gmail and the Google Display Network, and can reach more than 3 billion monthly active users. The logic is very close to Meta ads: people are not searching for anything, they meet your product while watching a video or scrolling a feed.

Over the past year Demand Gen has become Google’s main demand creation product. Google says the last Video action campaigns were automatically upgraded to Demand Gen by April 2026, and eligible advertisers can move Display campaigns with a migration tool from June 2026. If you want sales-focused ads on YouTube today, Demand Gen is the way in.

How is Demand Gen different from Performance Max?

Both run on several Google surfaces, but they do different jobs. Performance Max chases conversions across all of Google’s inventory, Search and Shopping included. Demand Gen does not show on search results; it focuses on visual feeds and gives you more control over creatives and audiences. I cover the search side in Performance Max or Shopping campaigns for e-commerce.

Can you choose where your ads show?

Yes. With channel controls you pick either “All Google channels” or “Let me choose” in each ad group. The second option lets you select YouTube in-stream, YouTube in-feed, YouTube Shorts, Discover, Gmail, Maps and the Display Network one by one. Google recommends all channels for most advertisers; if you want to force volume on one channel it suggests isolating it in its own ad group, while warning this may lower efficiency. Channel-level reporting is available at campaign, ad group and ad level.

How do you set up Demand Gen for e-commerce?

This is the order I follow in my own accounts:

  1. Check measurement. Demand Gen relies on Smart Bidding and is blind without purchase data. First set up Google Ads conversion tracking with sale values and confirm a test order is recorded once, with the right amount.
  2. Link your Merchant Center feed. Demand Gen supports product feeds, so product cards can appear under your video or image. Clean up disapproved Merchant Center products first.
  3. Pick the goal and bidding. Start with Maximize conversions or Maximize conversion value. Add target CPA or target ROAS once data builds up.
  4. Build audiences. Start with site visitors, customer lists or people who watched your YouTube channel, and use them as seeds for lookalike segments.
  5. Prepare creatives. Upload both video and images in the sizes below.
  6. Wait a few weeks. The campaign needs time to learn; don’t keep changing bids and targets in the first days.

Which creative sizes should you use?

Google’s Demand Gen asset specs recommend 1200×628 for landscape 1.91:1, 1200×1200 for square 1:1, 960×1200 for portrait 4:5 and 1080×1920 for vertical 9:16, which is recommended for Shorts. Images can be up to 5 MB; the logo is 1:1 and up to 150 KB. Videos can be as short as 5 seconds, but videos under 10 seconds cannot serve as YouTube in-stream ads.

There is a reason to combine video and images: Google says adding image assets to video campaigns brings on average 20% more conversions at the same CPA. That is Google’s average, so test it in your own account. Running the same creative for weeks can wear it out; I cover timing in ad fatigue and when to refresh an ad.

How much budget does Demand Gen need?

Google’s advice is clear: with target CPA, set a daily budget of at least 10 times your target CPA. If you want a sale at 250 TRY, that means at least 2,500 TRY a day. That can feel high for a small store, and it is meant to: a Demand Gen campaign launched on a tiny budget can’t learn and gets written off as “not working”. In its Video action upgrade guide Google even raises the ratio to 20 times for conversion-based bidding.

The bar for target ROAS is high too: Google’s target ROAS page asks for at least 50 conversions in the last 35 days for Demand Gen, 10 of them in the last 7 days. I explain how I choose the number in Google Ads target ROAS: how to set the right target. If you are deciding how to spread spend across channels, read how to split budget between Meta and Google Ads as well; Demand Gen often does the same job as Meta, so plan the two together.

What did Google add to Demand Gen in November 2026?

In its November Demand Gen Drop, published on November 18, 2026, Google announced four updates that matter for e-commerce: product feeds showing catalog items on YouTube, checkout links for a shorter path to purchase, a creator partnerships boost, and seasonality adjustments for target CPA or target ROAS in Demand Gen. The last one matters for sales events: it factors in the expected jump in conversion rate during short promotions, so the campaign bids competitively from day one and returns to baseline afterwards. I collected the other steps in Google Ads preparation for Black Friday.

Who should use Demand Gen?

It is a good growth channel for brands whose product is easy to show visually, who can produce video and whose search volume is still limited. For a store with a handful of sales a month, incomplete tracking or no creative production, it makes more sense to get Search and Shopping working first. Frequently asked questions

What is Google Ads Demand Gen?

Demand Gen is the Google Ads campaign type that runs image and video ads on YouTube, Shorts, Discover, Gmail and the Google Display Network. It is used to create interest among people who are not searching for you yet.

What is the difference between Demand Gen and Performance Max?

Performance Max looks for conversions across all of Google’s inventory, including Search and Shopping. Demand Gen does not show on search results; it focuses on visual feeds and gives more control over audiences, channels and creatives.

How much daily budget does Demand Gen need?

Google recommends a daily budget of at least 10 times your target CPA when you use target CPA. With a 250 TRY target CPA, that is at least 2,500 TRY a day.

Can I use target ROAS in Demand Gen?

Yes, but Google asks for at least 50 conversions in the last 35 days for Demand Gen, 10 of them in the last 7 days. With less data, start with Maximize conversion value.

Which image sizes do Demand Gen ads need?

Google recommends 1200×628 (1.91:1), 1200×1200 (1:1), 960×1200 (4:5) and 1080×1920 (9:16) for Shorts. Videos must be at least 5 seconds; videos under 10 seconds don’t serve as in-stream ads.
