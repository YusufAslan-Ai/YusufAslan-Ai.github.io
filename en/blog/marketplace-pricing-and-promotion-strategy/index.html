Pricing and promotion strategy on marketplaces

By Yusuf Aslan · 5 min read · October 2026

Net profit matters, not the sale price

From the sale price subtract commission, shipping, packaging, ads, returns and tax. What remains is your real profit. Many sellers grow revenue while losing money because they never do this calculation per product.

Don't just copy competitors

A competitor's price reflects their costs, not yours. Instead of cutting price, strengthen your product page, images and delivery speed. Being the cheapest does not always mean winning.

Check before joining a promotion

  • Is your margin still positive at the promo price?
  • Can your stock cover the promotional demand?
  • Can you return to the old price afterwards?

Split products into three groups

Not every product deserves the same treatment. One group pulls traffic and runs on thin margin. One group brings profit. One group is slow-moving stock. Make promotion and ad decisions according to the group.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my net profit on a marketplace?

Subtract commission, shipping, packaging, advertising, returns and tax from the sale price. What's left is your real profit, and you should calculate it per product.

Should I undercut a competitor's price?

No. Your competitor's price is based on their costs, not yours. Instead of cutting price, strengthen your product page, images and delivery speed.

What should I check before joining a marketplace promotion?

Whether your margin is still positive at the promotional price, whether stock can meet the demand, and whether you can return the price to its old level afterwards.
