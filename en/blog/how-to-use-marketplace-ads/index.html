Trendyol and Hepsiburada ads: where to start

By Yusuf Aslan · 5 min read · October 2026

Product page first, ads second

Ads bring traffic; the product page makes the sale. Ads on a product with a weak title, photo or price just burn budget. Before you advertise, make sure the product could sell organically too.

Which products deserve ads?

  • Products with sales history and positive reviews.
  • Products whose margin can carry the ad cost.
  • Products with enough stock. Advertising an out-of-stock product is the most expensive mistake.

Calculate your margin first

After ad spend, commission, shipping and returns, how much money is left? Setting targets without this is guessing. Know your break-even ad cost ratio and set the budget around it.

Start small, scale with data

The first weeks are a learning period. Start with a few products and a low daily budget, see which product and which search term brings sales, then raise the budget only on what works. Advertising the whole catalogue at once makes it impossible to tell what is working.
