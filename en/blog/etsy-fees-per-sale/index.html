Etsy fees in 2026: what Etsy takes from each sale

By Yusuf Aslan · 6 min read · November 2026

If you sell on Etsy from Türkiye, four core fees come out of every order: $0.20 per listing, a 6.5% transaction fee, a 6.5% + 14 TRY payment processing fee and a 1.67% regulatory operating fee. If your listing currency differs from your bank account’s currency, a 2.5% currency conversion fee is added, and if the sale came from Etsy’s Offsite Ads you pay up to 15% more. On a sale without ads, that is roughly 15–18% of the order total. Below I go through each fee using Etsy’s own pages and work out what is left from a $50 order.

Sellers who price without this calculation often find that revenue grows while savings don’t. The logic is the same as on any marketplace: take the fees out first, then set the price. I cover the general framework in marketplace pricing and promotion strategy; here I focus on what is specific to Etsy.

What fees does Etsy charge?

Here is everything from Etsy’s Fees & Payments Policy (last updated October 5, 2026) and the help pages that list country-specific rates:

FeeRate / amountCharged on
Listing fee$0.20Each listing; it expires after 4 months and renewing costs another $0.20
Transaction fee6.5%Item price plus shipping and gift wrap you charge
Payment processing (Türkiye)6.5% + 14 TRYOrder total, including shipping and tax
Regulatory operating fee (Türkiye)1.67%Item price plus shipping (and gift wrap or personalization)
Currency conversion2.5%Sale amount, when listing currency differs from payout currency
Offsite Ads15% (12% above the threshold)Only orders attributed to Etsy’s external ads; capped at $100 per order
Etsy AdsCost-per-click bids, daily budgetOptional on-Etsy advertising

The Türkiye rates come from separate pages: payment processing from Etsy’s payment processing fees page, and the 1.67% from the regulatory operating fee page. According to Etsy Fee Basics, if a buyer purchases several units from a multi-quantity listing, each additional unit also carries a $0.20 listing fee.

What is different for a seller in Türkiye?

  • Payment processing: 6.5% + 14 TRY for sellers with a bank account in Türkiye. It is separate from the 6.5% transaction fee, is deducted before your deposit, and is credited back in proportion to any refund.
  • Regulatory operating fee: a fixed percentage charged to sellers in certain countries. Türkiye is 1.67%; on the same table the UK is 0.48% and France 1.14%.
  • Deposits: Etsy lists a 50 TRY minimum deposit, a 600 TRY fee threshold and a 42 TRY deposit fee for Türkiye. Check these terms in your own account before withdrawing small balances often.

Fees in the policy exclude VAT. Etsy’s page on VAT on seller fees includes Türkiye among the countries where VAT may be collected on fees but gives no rate. Confirm how it applies to your business with your accountant; this is not tax advice.

What comes out of a $50 sale? A worked example

Assumptions: a $40 item plus $10 shipping charged to the buyer, $50 in total. You list in USD and your bank account is in TRY. The sale came from Etsy search, not from Offsite Ads.

FeeCalculationAmount
Listing feeFixed$0.20
Transaction fee$50 × 6.5%$3.25
Payment processing$50 × 6.5% + 14 TRY$3.25 + 14 TRY
Regulatory operating fee$50 × 1.67%$0.84
Currency conversion$50 × 2.5%$1.25
Total$8.79 + 14 TRY (≈ 17.6%)

If the same order had come from an Offsite Ads click, another $50 × 15% = $7.50 would be deducted, bringing the total to $16.29 + 14 TRY, close to a third of the order. These figures exclude any VAT on fees and the real shipping cost you pay the carrier: the $10 shipping charge is not profit, it pays for the parcel.

How should you price on Etsy?

  1. Add up your unit cost: product, packaging and the real shipping cost.
  2. Add up the percentage fees: 6.5% + 6.5% + 1.67% = 14.67%, or 17.17% if you list in USD and withdraw in TRY.
  3. Add the fixed fees: $0.20 plus 14 TRY converted at today’s rate.
  4. Set a target profit in dollars, with a small buffer for exchange-rate swings.
  5. Apply the formula: order total = (cost + fixed fees + target profit) ÷ (1 − percentage fees). With a $20 cost, $10 target profit and the $0.20 listing fee: 30.20 ÷ 0.8283 ≈ $36.46, then add the dollar value of 14 TRY to the top of the fraction the same way.
  6. Run an Offsite Ads scenario by adding 15% to the fee rate. If ad-driven orders lose money, change either the price or your ad choice.

Can you avoid the Offsite Ads fee?

According to Etsy’s Offsite Ads page, Etsy advertises listings on sites and apps outside Etsy with its own budget. If a buyer clicks one of these ads and buys from your shop within 30 days, the order is attributed and the fee applies:

  • Shops that have never made $10,000 in any 365-day period pay 15% and can opt out.
  • Shops that have passed that threshold pay 12% but must take part for the lifetime of the shop.
  • The fee on a single order never exceeds $100, whatever the order total.

My advice: if your margins are thin and you are below the threshold, price for the no-ads scenario and consider opting out. If you are above it, you have no choice, so build 12% into the price from the start. Etsy Ads, the on-site cost-per-click ads, are fully optional and you set the budget. When picking which product to advertise first, the rule from where to start with marketplace ads applies on Etsy too: start with a profitable product that already has good reviews.

How can you reduce what Etsy takes?

  • Review the conversion fee. If your listing currency matches your payout currency, the 2.5% fee does not apply. Weigh that against how prices look to buyers and your exchange-rate risk.
  • Don’t auto-renew listings that never sell. Each renewal is another $0.20. Refresh the title, photos and price before relisting a product that sat unsold for four months.
  • Raise conversion. More sales per listing lowers the fixed cost per unit. See Etsy listing SEO: titles, tags and photos and my guide to marketplace product images and descriptions.
  • Compare channels. If your product is standard rather than handmade, work out the costs on Amazon too with my checklist for getting your first product ready to sell on Amazon.

Frequently asked questions

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. Under Etsy’s fee policy the 6.5% transaction fee also applies to the shipping and gift-wrap price you charge the buyer, and payment processing is calculated on the order total including shipping. Set your shipping price so it covers the fees, not just the carrier cost.

How much does Etsy take in total from a seller in Türkiye?

On a sale without Offsite Ads, the percentage fees add up to 14.67% (6.5% transaction, 6.5% payment processing, 1.67% regulatory operating fee), plus $0.20 for the listing and a fixed 14 TRY. Listing in a currency other than your payout currency adds 2.5%. An Offsite Ads order adds 15% or 12%. VAT may apply to the fees.

Can I opt out of Etsy Offsite Ads?

Yes, if your shop has never made $10,000 or more in any 365-day period. Once a shop passes that threshold, Etsy requires it to stay in Offsite Ads for the lifetime of the shop, at a reduced 12% fee.

Does it cost money to open an Etsy shop?

Etsy’s fee policy describes a one-time set-up fee that is non-refundable once paid, but the policy does not state the amount, so check what is shown during shop setup. After that, each listing costs $0.20.
