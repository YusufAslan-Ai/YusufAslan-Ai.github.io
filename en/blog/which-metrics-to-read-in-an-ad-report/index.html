Which metrics to read in an ad report

By Yusuf Aslan · 4 min read · October 2026

Not every number helps

Likes, impressions and reach look nice but do not show that you earned money. First answer this: how much sales did my ad spend bring?

These four numbers first

  • ROAS: revenue for every unit of currency spent.
  • Cost per purchase: what a sale costs you.
  • conversion-rate/">Conversion rate: how many of those who click buy.
  • Average order value: revenue per sale.

Then find the cause

If one of those worsens, look at click-through rate, cost per click and frequency to find why. If click-through falls, the creative is the problem; if conversion rate falls, the page or the offer is.

Compare with your own order data

Sales counted in the ad dashboard may not match real orders. Keep both in view while reading the report and know the gap between them.
