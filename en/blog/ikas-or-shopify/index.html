ikas or Shopify? Choosing an e-commerce platform

By Yusuf Aslan · 7 min read · November 2026

If most of your sales come from Türkiye and you also sell on marketplaces like Trendyol and Hepsiburada, ikas is usually the more practical choice: lira-priced plans, a built-in virtual POS and local marketplace integrations come in one panel. If you mainly sell abroad, especially through a company registered in a country where Shopify Payments is available, Shopify’s large app ecosystem pulls ahead. Make the call on payments, marketplaces and target market, not on which theme looks nicer. Below I compare the two platforms using each company’s own official pages.

What is the core difference between ikas and Shopify?

ikas is an e-commerce platform built in Türkiye; Shopify is a global platform headquartered in Canada. On paper that sounds minor, but day to day you feel it in three places: how you take payments, how you connect to marketplaces and which currency your monthly bill is in.

ikas’s pricing page (in Turkish) lists plans in Turkish lira and includes a free Start plan. Shopify’s pricing page is in US dollars: the Basic plan is $39 a month billed monthly, or $29 a month billed yearly. With a moving exchange rate, a fixed lira budget is harder to hold on Shopify, so factor that in.

Which one is better for taking payments?

This is the most overlooked part of the comparison. Shopify’s list of supported countries for Shopify Payments has 41 countries, and Türkiye is not one of them. If you open a Shopify store with a Turkish company, you need a third-party payment provider, and Shopify then charges its own transaction fee on top of the provider’s rate:

Basic$392%
Grow$1051%
Advanced$3990.6%
PlusFrom $2,3000.2%

A simple example: at 200,000 TRY in monthly revenue on Basic, you pay 4,000 TRY in Shopify transaction fees alone, before any card processing cost. That can be several times the subscription itself, so put it in your cost sheet when you talk about profitability.

On the ikas side, the pricing page says every plan includes a ready-made virtual POS and that you can connect your own bank POS at no extra charge. The ready-made POS rate depends on how fast you want the money (the settlement period), and the page shows different rates for different periods. Rates change often, so confirm the current figures with ikas in writing before you sign.

Which should you pick if you also sell on marketplaces?

Many Turkish brands make a large share of revenue on marketplaces, so stock and order sync between your site and those channels becomes central to the decision. ikas’s marketplace integrations page says orders and stock can be kept in sync for Trendyol, Hepsiburada, Etsy and Amazon, and that the Scale Plus and Premium plans give access to 19 domestic and 7 international marketplaces. Which integration sits in which plan is worded differently across ikas’s pages, so ask before you buy.

Shopify’s own Marketplace Connect is built for Amazon, Target Plus, Walmart and eBay. To reach Trendyol or Hepsiburada from Shopify you need a third-party app or integrator, which means another monthly fee and one more link that can break. If you are new to marketplaces, first get clear on the steps to open a Hepsiburada store, then check how the integration covers them.

Is Shopify the better choice for selling abroad?

Usually yes, with one condition. Shopify’s real strength is its international themes, multi-language and multi-currency options and a very large app store. If your company is registered where Shopify Payments works (for example the US, the UK or an EU country), the extra transaction fee disappears and Shopify becomes far more competitive.

If your company is only in Türkiye and international sales are still a small share, keeping your main site on a local platform and going abroad through marketplaces is often the lower-risk start. My view: choose the platform for today’s revenue mix, not for the plan you have for two years from now. You can always migrate later, as long as you redirect your URLs properly.

Is there a difference in SEO and ad tracking?

For basic technical SEO, both platforms do most of the work automatically: the sitemap and robots.txt are generated for you. The difference is in the fields you have to fill in. I go through what to check in the admin panel in my ikas SEO settings guide and in my article on Shopify SEO settings. Whichever you choose, leaving titles and descriptions on autopilot costs you the same on both.

For a store that advertises, tracking matters even more. Making sure the purchase event fires once, with the right value, is something you have to test yourself on any platform. Re-test your Meta Pixel and Conversions API setup after any platform switch; the most common post-migration problem I see is purchases counted twice or not at all.

What steps should you follow before deciding?

  1. Write down your revenue mix. What share of sales comes, or will come, from Türkiye versus abroad?
  2. List your channels. Which marketplaces do you sell on? Confirm which plan includes each integration.
  3. Add up the total cost. Subscription + platform transaction fee + card processing + paid apps. On Shopify, add the exchange rate risk.
  4. Check the must-have integrations. See e-invoicing, shipping and accounting connections working in a demo account.
  5. Plan 301 redirects if you migrate. Don’t go live before every old product and category URL points to its new one.
  6. Place a test order. Use the free plan or trial to run a real order end to end: payment, invoice, shipping and ad tracking.

If you are moving an existing store to a new platform, don’t start before planning URL mapping and 301 redirects; I walk through the steps in my guide to switching e-commerce platforms without losing SEO.

Frequently asked questions

Is ikas or Shopify better?

If most of your sales come from Türkiye and you also sell on marketplaces, ikas is usually more practical. If you mainly sell abroad through a company in a country where Shopify Payments is available, Shopify pulls ahead.

Can I use Shopify in Türkiye?

Yes, but Shopify Payments is not offered in Türkiye, so you need a third-party payment provider, and Shopify then charges an extra transaction fee of 2% to 0.2% depending on your plan.

How much is the Shopify transaction fee?

According to Shopify’s pricing page, the fee with a third-party payment provider is 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow, 0.6% on Advanced and 0.2% on Plus. It comes on top of the provider’s own rate.

Does ikas integrate with Trendyol and Hepsiburada?

Yes. ikas’s marketplace integrations page says orders and stock can be synced for Trendyol, Hepsiburada, Etsy and Amazon. Confirm which integration is included in which plan before you buy.

What should I watch when migrating between Shopify and ikas?

The most important step is 301-redirecting every old product and category URL to its new one. Then test payment, invoicing, shipping and ad tracking end to end with a real order.
