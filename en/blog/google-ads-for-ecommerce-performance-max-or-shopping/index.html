Google Ads for e-commerce: Performance Max or Shopping campaigns?

By Yusuf Aslan · 5 min read · October 2026

Both depend on your product feed

Both campaign types use the product data in Merchant Center. If titles, images, prices or stock are wrong, results will be poor whichever type you pick. Fix the feed first.

Shopping campaigns: control

Products appear as cards in search results. You manage more clearly which product gets how much budget. A good start for stores with a small catalogue that want control.

Performance Max: reach

It serves automatically across Google surfaces (search, shopping, YouTube, display). It brings more reach but makes it harder to see what each surface delivers. It works better in accounts with enough conversion data.

Practical advice

With little data, start with Shopping, then test Performance Max once conversion data builds up. Avoid making the two compete on the same products, and do not decide before watching results for at least two weeks. That is why you should settle your campaign structure well before big sales; I collected the steps for Black Friday Google Ads preparation in a separate post.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Performance Max and Shopping campaigns?

Shopping campaigns appear as product cards in search results and give clearer control over budget. Performance Max shows ads automatically across all Google surfaces, with wider reach but less transparency.

Which should a new e-commerce store start with?

If conversion data is limited, start with a Shopping campaign. Test Performance Max as data builds up, and don't let the two compete on the same products.

What should I fix before choosing a campaign type?

Your product feed in Merchant Center. Both campaign types use it; if titles, images, prices or stock data are wrong, results will be poor whichever type you pick.
