What are the basic SEO settings in your Ideasoft store?

By Yusuf Aslan · 4 min read · October 2026

The platform does not matter, the basics are the same

Whichever e-commerce platform you use, what a geo-checklist/">search engine sees is similar: page title, description, URL, heading structure, images and content. You find where the settings live in your own panel; this article covers what to check.

Unique title and description on every page

Check for automatically generated or repeated titles on product and category pages. Hundreds of pages with the same title tell a search engine you do not know what they are about.

Readable URLs

Short URLs that describe the product or category help both users and search engines. If you change a URL, remember to redirect the old one to the new one.

Add text to category pages

Add a short, meaningful introduction to category pages that only list products. Category pages are often the least used SEO opportunity in e-commerce.
