How to open a Hepsiburada store: a step-by-step guide

By Yusuf Aslan · 6 min read · November 2026

Opening a store on Hepsiburada is free: in the İş Ortağım seller panel you click “Yeni başvuru” (new application), choose a seller type, set a store name and password, complete any missing details and approve the contract. A standard application needs no document upload, because your company records are pulled in automatically. Once the review is done, your account goes live and you get an email. The real work starts after that. If you list products without accounting for commission, shipping and ad costs, you can make sales and still lose money. Below I walk through the application and what to check before your first sale.

Hepsiburada is one of Türkiye’s largest marketplaces. Its official seller page cites 210 million monthly visits, 12 million active customers and more than 101,000 active stores. In a market that crowded, opening the store is the easy part; listing products at the right price with the right content is what makes the difference.

Who can open a store on Hepsiburada?

You pick one of three seller types during the application. The choice decides which benefits you can use and which extra documents you may need.

Seller typeWho it is forExtra documents
StandardAny seller that doesn’t fit the other two typesNot required
Women Entrepreneur (Girişimci Kadın)Businesses where a woman holds 51% or more of the sharesRequired (Hesabım > Belgelerim)
HepsiyürektenFoundations, associations and similar non-profitsDepends on the case

Hepsiburada also asks for extra documents in special cases such as ordinary partnerships. If you are unsure, Standard is the right starting point for most businesses.

How do you open a Hepsiburada store, step by step?

  1. Prepare your details. Have your company name, tax details, bank account and contact information ready. Company records are fetched automatically, but you fill in the gaps.
  2. Start the application. In the İş Ortağım seller panel, click “Yeni başvuru”, choose your seller type and create a store name and password. Pick a name customers will remember and that matches your brand.
  3. Complete missing details and documents. Fill in the empty fields under Hesabım > Bilgilerim and, for special seller types, upload documents under Hesabım > Belgelerim.
  4. Approve the contract. Once your details are verified, you approve the contract with one click on the application screen.
  5. Track the review. You can follow the status in the panel. If a document is rejected, you get an email explaining why, then fix and re-upload it.
  6. List your first products. Instead of opening your whole catalogue at once, start with 10–20 products whose margin and content you have checked.

Is it free, and how much commission does Hepsiburada take?

Opening the store costs nothing. Hepsiburada takes a category-based commission on each sale; current rates are published in Hepsiburada’s commission rate file, and shipping fees by volumetric weight (desi) sit in a separate file. Check your own category’s row before pricing, rather than relying on a rate someone else quoted, because the rates are updated from time to time.

What do new sellers get?

The “Efsane Hoş Geldin Paketi” described on Hepsiburada’s page gives new stores a 50% commission discount (a 10% + VAT rate) for 90 days after opening; it does not apply to electronics. Women entrepreneurs get a 5,000 TL Sponsored Product ad balance within 7 days of their first product going live. Note that the page lists 31 October 2026 as the deadline to join. That date had passed when I wrote this, so check the panel for a new period before you apply.

Shipping can come with performance-based support too. In a model announced in July 2025, sellers who ship on time were offered up to 54% off shipping on orders up to 150 TL. Terms like these change, so always plan with the current ones.

How should you price before you go live?

The most common mistake I see is listing at your website price and only noticing the deductions later. A simple example (hypothetical figures; use your category’s real rates): you sell at 600 TL with a 20% commission, which is 120 TL. With 60 TL shipping, 250 TL product cost and 15 TL packaging, you keep 155 TL. Add returns and ad spend and the margin shrinks fast.

So find your break-even point first, then add room for promotions on top. I cover how to protect your margin during campaigns in pricing and promotion strategy on marketplaces. In busy periods like November, this calculation matters even more.

What should you check when listing your first products?

  • Title: Put brand, product type, key feature and variant in a clear order. The pattern in how to write a marketplace product title works here too.
  • Images and description: A white-background main image, in-use photos and a description that answers questions upfront reduce returns. See marketplace product images and descriptions for details.
  • Stock and dispatch time: Enter a handling time you can actually keep. Late shipments hurt both reviews and store performance; the logic is the same as in raising your Trendyol seller score.

If you also run your own website, using your platform’s marketplace integration instead of entering products by hand cuts stock errors. I explain this with Ticimax as the example in marketplace integration in Ticimax.

What should you focus on in the first 90 days?

  1. First two weeks: Ship on time, answer customer questions quickly and collect your first reviews.
  2. First month: Watch which products get clicks but no sales, and fix price, images or title product by product.
  3. Months two and three: Start low-budget Sponsored Product ads on products you know are profitable. I explain where to begin in Trendyol and Hepsiburada ads.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to open a store on Hepsiburada?

Yes, opening a store is free. Hepsiburada takes a category-based commission on each sale, and shipping fees are calculated separately by volumetric weight. Check the current rates in Hepsiburada’s commission and shipping files.

What documents do I need to apply to Hepsiburada?

A standard seller application needs no document upload; after you enter your basic details, your company records are fetched automatically. Special cases such as Women Entrepreneur or ordinary partnerships require extra documents under Hesabım > Belgelerim.

How long does Hepsiburada take to approve a store?

Hepsiburada does not publish a fixed timeframe. Your application is reviewed after your details and contract approval are complete, you can track it in the seller panel, and you get an email once the account is active.

Do new sellers get a commission discount?

Hepsiburada’s Efsane Hoş Geldin Paketi gives new stores a 50% commission discount (a 10% + VAT rate) for 90 days, excluding electronics. The page lists 31 October 2026 as the deadline to join, so check the panel for the current offer before applying.
