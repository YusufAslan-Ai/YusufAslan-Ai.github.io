Ad fatigue: when should you refresh an ad?

By Yusuf Aslan · 3 min read · October 2026

When the same image and copy run for weeks, performance slowly drops. This is called ad fatigue. The signs:

  • Frequency is rising while click-through rate is falling.
  • Cost per click is rising and sales are falling for the same spend.
  • Comments start to read like "I've seen this too many times".

What to do?

Build the new ad with the same offer but a different angle: a new first sentence (hook), a new visual, a customer review or a short video. Testing a small new version every week is safer than making one big change a month.

Frequently asked questions

What is ad fatigue?

The gradual drop in performance when the same image and copy run for weeks. The audience has seen the ad so often that it stops responding.

What are the signs of ad fatigue?

Frequency rising, click-through rate falling, cost per click rising and fewer sales for the same spend. Comments that feel like "I've seen this too many times" are another sign.

What should I do when an ad is fatigued?

Present the same offer from a new angle: a new hook, a new image, a customer review or a short video. Testing a small new version every week is safer than one big change a month.
