Advantage+ or manual campaign?

By Yusuf Aslan · 4 min read · October 2026

Advantage+ campaigns leave most targeting and placement decisions to Meta's algorithm. In a manual campaign, you decide them. They aren't rivals; they do different jobs.

Advantage+ works well

when the account has enough conversion data, when you sell a catalog with many products, or when you want to scale to a broad audience.

Manual is better

for a new account with little data, a very specific audience (a local business, a certain age group), or when you want to run a clean test.

A practical method: test the creative and the offer in a manual campaign, then scale the winner with Advantage+. Decide which is better by the ROAS in your own account, not by guessing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Advantage+ and a manual campaign?

In an Advantage+ campaign, Meta's algorithm makes most targeting and placement decisions. In a manual campaign, you choose the audience, placements and setup yourself.

Which should I start with on a new ad account?

If the account doesn't have enough conversion data yet, a manual campaign is the better fit. You test creative and offers in a controlled way and build up data.

When does Advantage+ work better?

When the account has enough conversion data, you sell many products from a catalog and you want to scale to a broad audience. Decide with the ROAS in your own account, not by guessing.
