How to prepare product images and descriptions for marketplaces

By Yusuf Aslan · 4 min read · October 2026

The first photo decides the click

In search results the customer sees a small image first. A clean background, the whole product in frame and good light win the first click. Too much text or framing over the photo hurts readability and can break platform rules.

Let the image order tell a story

  • Image 1: the product alone, sharp.
  • Images 2-3: different angles and a close-up of details.
  • Image 4: size and scale comparison.
  • Image 5: in use.

Keep the description short and scannable

People scan; they do not read long text. Give material, size, use and care in short points. This reduces returns and helps the product match more searches.

Avoid exaggerated claims

"The best" or promises you cannot prove come back to you in reviews and returns. Trust grows when a seller is accurate and clear, and trust lifts conversion.

Frequently asked questions

What should the first product photo on a marketplace look like?

A clean background, the whole product visible and good lighting. Adding lots of text or frames hurts readability and can break platform rules.

In what order should I upload product images?

Image 1: the product alone; images 2–3: other angles and close-up detail; image 4: size comparison; image 5: the product in use.

How should a product description be written?

Short and scannable. Give material, size, usage and care information in brief bullet points; this reduces returns and helps the product match searches.
