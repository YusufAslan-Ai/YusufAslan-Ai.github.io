ikas SEO settings: 7 things to check in your store

By Yusuf Aslan · 6 min read · November 2026

ikas SEO settings live in one place: the “SEO & Domain” section under the B2C sales channel, where you manage the homepage title and description, meta templates for products and categories, 301/302 redirects and your sitemap. ikas builds and updates the sitemap and robots.txt for you and compresses images automatically. Your job is to write the text that shouldn’t be left on autopilot and to keep old URLs alive when you migrate. Work through the seven checks below and your store’s SEO foundation will be solid.

ikas is a Turkish e-commerce platform, and in store setups I see the same pattern again and again: theme, payments and shipping get all the attention while the titles search engines read stay on their defaults. If you run a different platform, the same logic applies in my guides to Shopify SEO settings and Ideasoft store SEO settings.

What does ikas handle automatically for SEO?

Start by knowing what you don’t need to touch. According to ikas’s own SEO features page (in Turkish):

  • The sitemap is generated automatically and updates as you add products.
  • robots.txt is created automatically, and you can edit it manually if needed.
  • Uploaded images are optimized and converted to WebP.
  • An SSL certificate is included at no extra cost.
  • Product, category and blog pages have title and meta description fields, and there’s no extra fee for SEO settings.

So the technical base is in place. But an “automatic” title is usually just the product name, and it gives a searcher no reason to click your result. The real difference comes from the manual settings below.

How do you set up ikas SEO in 7 steps?

  1. Write the homepage title and description. In SEO & Domain > SEO and Image Preview.
  2. Edit the meta title and description templates. Separately for products, brands, categories and blog.
  3. Hand-write titles for your best sellers and main categories. Templates give you a baseline; key pages need custom copy.
  4. Keep product URLs clean. Short, descriptive slugs without special characters.
  5. Set up 301 redirects if you’re migrating. Ideally before you connect your domain.
  6. Hide low-value pages with noindex. Thank-you pages, test pages or empty categories.
  7. Connect Google Search Console and submit your sitemap.

Where do you change the homepage title and description in ikas?

ikas’s guide on editing your homepage’s appearance in search results gives the path: Sales Channel B2C > SEO and Domain > SEO and Image Preview. Pick the language and edit the “SEO Page Title” and “SEO Description” fields. You upload your favicon in the same section; JPEG, PNG and WebP are accepted, up to 10 MB.

A concrete example: instead of “StoreName | Home”, use “Genuine Leather Bags and Wallets | StoreName” so the search term is in the title. In the description, give a reason to click, such as free shipping, the return window or where the products are made. Google’s SEO Starter Guide also recommends titles that are concise and describe the page, and descriptions that summarize it.

How do meta title templates work?

Writing hundreds of product titles by hand isn’t realistic, so ikas offers templates. Its meta title and description templates guide explains that “Edit Templates” in SEO & Domain lets you set separate templates for Products, Brands, Categories, Blog and Blog Categories. Variables sit below the fields and can be inserted with a click, with a live preview on the right. Leave a field empty and the default applies: the page’s own name becomes the title, and the description is generated from the product text.

The structure I suggest: “product name + brand + store name” for titles, and “product name + one benefit + shipping/returns info” for descriptions. Then open your 20 to 30 top-selling products and main categories one by one and write custom titles. I explain why category pages deserve extra effort in category pages and e-commerce SEO: broad searches usually land on a category, not a single product.

How do you set up redirects when moving to ikas?

The biggest SEO loss in a platform migration is old product URLs turning into error pages. Google’s redirects documentation recommends a permanent (301) redirect when a page has moved for good. The steps from ikas’s 301/302 redirects guide:

  1. Go to Sales Channel B2C > SEO & Domain > Redirects and click “Edit Redirects”.
  2. Click “Add Redirect”.
  3. Enter the old and new URL, using only the path after the domain (for example /contact).
  4. Leave the “302 Redirect” box unchecked so it’s a permanent 301.
  5. Save.

For large catalogs, use bulk redirect import: export the template as CSV or XLS from the same screen, put old URLs in column two and new URLs in column three, add TRUE in column four only for temporary redirects, then import the file. ikas recommends setting up redirects before connecting your domain where possible, and the panel doesn’t support redirects for slugs with special characters. So before you migrate, export every product and category URL from the old site into a sheet and flag the problem ones.

How do you submit the ikas sitemap to Search Console?

Following ikas’s sitemap submission guide, first create a “Domain” property in Search Console. Add the TXT record Google gives you to your domain provider’s DNS settings; verification can take from a few minutes to several hours. Then go to Indexing > Sitemaps, enter yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml and submit. Multilingual stores get a separate sitemap per language, such as sitemap-en.xml. A “Failed” status on the first submission is normal and should switch to “Success” after a refresh a few minutes later.

One important note from the guide: edit the meta information for your category, brand and theme pages and make sure the store is live before you submit. After that, check the “Pages” report once a month; unindexed products and old URLs returning 404 show up there.

What else matters on the product page?

Panel settings are the foundation; the copy is what ranks. Instead of pasting the supplier’s description, answer the size, material, usage, shipping and returns questions in your own words. Check your theme’s structured data too, so price and stock reach Google correctly; I cover the key fields in structured data on product pages. Clear, question-and-answer product copy also improves your chances of showing up in AI answers like Google AI Mode.

Frequently asked questions

Is ikas good for SEO?

The technical base is ready: the sitemap and robots.txt are automatic, images are converted to WebP, SSL is free and 301/302 redirects are in the panel. What really moves rankings is the titles, descriptions and product copy you write.

Where are the SEO settings in ikas?

In the SEO & Domain section under the B2C sales channel. The homepage title, meta templates, redirects and favicon are managed there; custom product and category copy goes in each page’s SEO fields.

What is the ikas sitemap URL?

It sits at the root of your domain, yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml, and updates automatically. Multilingual stores have a separate sitemap per language, such as sitemap-en.xml.

Will my rankings drop when I move to ikas?

If you 301-redirect old URLs to their new addresses, you avoid most of the loss. ikas recommends setting up redirects before connecting your domain where possible, and supports bulk import via CSV or XLS.
