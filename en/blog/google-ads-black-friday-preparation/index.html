Google Ads Black Friday prep: a 7-step checklist

By Yusuf Aslan · 6 min read · November 2026

Preparing Google Ads for Black Friday is not about launching campaigns on the day; it is about fixing tracking, product prices, promotions and budgets at least two weeks in advance. This year Black Friday falls on Friday, 27 November 2026. Work through the seven steps below in order and your budget won’t disappear into places you can’t predict during sale week.

In Turkey, November sales no longer squeeze into a single day; many brands run promotions across the whole month. So treat these checks as work to finish now, not last-week tasks.

What should you check first in Google Ads before Black Friday?

Measurement. When traffic and sales jump, a conversion tag that misses or double-counts orders pushes Smart Bidding in the wrong direction. Make sure purchases are counted once, order values come through correctly and a test order shows up in reports. For a step-by-step list, see setting up Google Ads conversion tracking correctly.

How do you prepare Google Ads for Black Friday in 7 steps?

  1. Verify conversion tracking. Place a test order and confirm the conversion and order value reach Google Ads.
  2. Add dated sale prices to your feed. Send the sale price [sale_price] attribute together with the sale price effective date [sale_price_effective_date] in Merchant Center, so prices switch on when the sale starts and revert when it ends.
  3. Match the landing page price to the feed. If the feed shows a sale price and the site shows the old one, products can be disapproved; I list the other common causes in what to do when Merchant Center disapproves products. Your product page structured data should show the same price too.
  4. Add a promotion asset. Set up the line that shows your discount under search ads, with the Black Friday occasion.
  5. Plan budgets from a forecast. Use Performance Planner and raise daily budgets gradually.
  6. Use seasonality adjustments only for short, sharp spikes. Set one only if a few-day sale will clearly lift your conversion rate.
  7. Watch search terms every day. Irrelevant searches grow during sales; keep your negative keyword list current.

How do you add sale prices in Merchant Center?

The cleanest way is to send the sale price and its effective dates in your feed instead of editing prices by hand. For a product normally priced at 1,000 TL, a sale price of 799 TL with an effective date of 23–30 November shows the discount in that window and switches back automatically. You no longer risk forgetting to update the feed the night the sale ends.

If Merchant Center promotions are available in your account (offers like “20% off” or “100 TL off orders over 500 TL”), submit those early as well. Google says promotion review is usually completed within 24 hours of the start time but can take up to 72 hours, so leaving it to the last day is risky. If you also run the sale on marketplaces, keep prices consistent with the ideas in marketplace pricing and promotion strategy.

What does a promotion asset do on Black Friday?

A promotion asset shows your discount (as a percentage or an amount) under your search ad with a bold occasion label. According to Google:

  • The Black Friday and Cyber Monday occasions run from 15 October to 15 December; you can narrow that window but not go outside it.
  • You can add it at account, campaign or ad group level; the most specific level wins.
  • It is free to add; a click costs the same as a headline click.
  • Editing the asset does not reset its performance statistics.

My practical advice: put clear sale dates in the asset and send people straight to the category with the discounted products, not to the home page where they have to hunt for the deal.

How do you plan a Google Ads budget for Black Friday?

Don’t plan the budget as “double what we spent last year”; plan it from a forecast. Performance Planner builds forecasts from the last 7–10 days of data adjusted for seasonality, and it supports Search, Shopping, Performance Max and Demand Gen campaigns. Before raising spend, put margin, cost per order and return rate side by side; the 3 numbers to check before raising your ad budget is a good starting point.

Also know this rule: according to Google, most campaigns can spend up to twice the average daily budget on a single day, but you won’t be charged more than 30.4 times the daily budget in a month. A campaign with a 1,000 TL daily budget may spend 2,000 TL on Black Friday; that is normal, so don’t panic and pause it.

When should you use seasonality adjustments?

Seasonality adjustments are an advanced tool that lets you tell Smart Bidding in advance how much your conversion rate will change on certain dates. Google’s own guidance is clear:

  • They are ideal for short events of 1–7 days and may not work as well for periods longer than 14 days.
  • Use them only when you expect a major change in conversion rate; Smart Bidding already handles smaller seasonal swings.
  • They work with Target ROAS or Target CPA on Search, Shopping, Demand Gen and Display, and with all bid strategies on Performance Max.

So they suit the core sale days, say 27–30 November, not a campaign that runs all month. Base the value on last year’s conversion rate change for the same period, not on a guess.

Which mistakes should you avoid during sale week?

The most common mistake I see is rebuilding the campaign structure in the final week. Launching new campaigns or switching bid strategies starts a new learning phase exactly when you need stability. Decide between Shopping and Performance Max now; the comparison in Performance Max or Shopping campaigns will help.

The second is ignoring search terms. Searches like “when is Black Friday”, “free” or competitor brand names grow in this period and take clicks without selling. Check the search terms report daily and update your negative keyword list.

The third is leaving everything as it is after the sale. Set end dates for promotion assets, budgets and seasonality adjustments in advance, so no ad is still promising a discount at old prices on the morning of 1 December.

Frequently asked questions

When should I start preparing Google Ads for Black Friday?

At least two to three weeks ahead. Checking tracking, adding sale prices to your feed and getting Merchant Center promotions approved all take time; keep the final week for monitoring and small tweaks.

How much should I raise my budget for Black Friday?

There is no single right ratio. Get a forecast from Performance Planner, work out the cost per click your margin can carry and raise budgets gradually. Remember that most campaigns can spend up to twice the average daily budget on a single day.

Should I use seasonality adjustments on every campaign?

No. Google recommends them only for short events of 1–7 days where you expect a major change in conversion rate. For small promotions, Smart Bidding already accounts for seasonal patterns.

Do promotion assets cost extra?

Adding them is free. If someone clicks, you pay the same as a headline click. With the Black Friday occasion selected, the asset can run between 15 October and 15 December.
