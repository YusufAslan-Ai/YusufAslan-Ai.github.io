How to split your budget between Meta and Google ads

By Yusuf Aslan · 5 min read · October 2026

The two channels do different jobs

On Google a person is searching, so you capture existing demand. On Meta you appear while they scroll, so you create demand. Neither works at full efficiency without the other.

A simple starting rule

If brand awareness is low, put more weight on Meta; if people already search for your brand and products, weight Google. There is no fixed percentage; your own data decides.

Weigh both channels on one scale

Each channel’s report flatters itself. For a fair comparison, combine total orders and total ad spend in one place, such as your own order system.

Shift budget gradually

If one channel is consistently better, do not move the whole budget there. Shift a small part each time and watch the result, because efficiency can drop as you raise a channel’s budget.
