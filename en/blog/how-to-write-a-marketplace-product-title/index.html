How to write a marketplace product title

By Yusuf Aslan · 4 min read · October 2026

On Trendyol and Hepsiburada, the product title affects both search ranking and click-through rate. A short, generic title won't rank, and an overlong title stuffed with keywords lowers trust.

A simple formula

Brand + product type + distinguishing feature + use case or size/color. Example: "Brand X Orthopedic Mattress 160x200 Double Medium Firm".

  • Use the words buyers actually type, not fancy names you invented.
  • Put the most important keyword in the first half of the title.
  • Don't repeat the same word; marketplaces treat it as noise.
  • Also fill in product attributes (material, size, color) in their own fields, not only in the title. Missing fields lower your ranking.

After changing a title, wait at least two weeks and compare impressions and click-through rate. If you change several things at once, you won't know what worked.

Frequently asked questions

How should a Trendyol product title be written?

A simple formula: brand + product type + distinguishing feature + use case or size/color. Put the most important keyword in the first half of the title.

Does adding lots of keywords to a title help?

No. An overly long, keyword-stuffed title lowers trust, and repeating the same word doesn't help on marketplaces.

When will I see the result of a title change?

Wait at least two weeks after the change and compare impressions and click-through rate with the previous period. Don't change anything else at the same time.
