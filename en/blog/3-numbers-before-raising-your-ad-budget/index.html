3 numbers to check before raising your ad budget

By Yusuf Aslan · 4 min read · October 2026

When sales drop, the first reflex is to raise the budget. But money added to a broken ad only widens the leak. Look at these first:

ROAS (return on ad spend)

Sales for every lira you spend. Don't set a target ROAS without knowing your profit margin. The break-even point is 1 divided by your margin.

Breakdown by ad set and ad

Which ad gets most of the spend, and which one brings most of the sales? In most accounts, about a third of the ads carry the sales and the rest only spend.

Frequency

If the same person sees the ad too often, the audience is tired. Raising the budget makes this worse.

The rule is simple: find the winner first, then raise its budget gradually. Shut down the loser and move the money to the winner. Large, sudden increases can disrupt learning, so raise the budget in a few steps.
