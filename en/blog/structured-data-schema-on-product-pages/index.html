Why structured data (schema) matters on product pages

By Yusuf Aslan · 3 min read · October 2026

Structured data tells search engines and AI systems in machine language what is on your page: this is a product, this is its price, it's in stock, this is its rating.

Product

Product name, images, brand, description.

Offer

Price, currency, stock status. It must match the price on the site exactly.

AggregateRating and Review

Only for real reviews. Made-up ratings violate search engine policies.

FAQPage and Organization

Defines frequently asked questions and brand information.

After adding it, validate with Google's Rich Results Test. Wrong markup can do worse than no markup at all.
