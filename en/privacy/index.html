Privacy & Cookie Policy

What data I process and why, the cookies I use, and your rights.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This page explains what data is processed when you visit yusufaslann.com or fill in the application form, and why, in line with Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Data controller

Yusuf Aslan. For any request about this policy or your data, message @yusufaslancom on Instagram.

What data I process and why

Application form

Name, brand or business name, Instagram account or website, WhatsApp number, optional email, the service you are interested in, budget range and your notes. This is used only to review your application and contact you, based on the explicit consent you give in the form. Responses are stored in Google Forms (Google LLC).

Analytics and advertising cookies (only with your consent)

  • Google Analytics 4 (via Google Tag Manager): statistics such as pages visited, visit duration, device and browser type and approximate location.
  • Meta Pixel: measuring Facebook and Instagram ads for site visitors and building ad audiences.
  • Microsoft Clarity: heatmaps and anonymous session recordings from clicks, scrolling and mouse movement. Anything you type into form fields is masked and not recorded.

If you don't consent, these tools set no cookies; Google Analytics can only send cookieless, anonymous aggregate pings. Your choice is stored in your browser and you can change it at any time.

Essential browser storage

Small settings such as your cookie choice and not showing the reminder box again are kept only in your browser (localStorage) and are not sent to me.

Transfers

The servers of Google LLC, Meta Platforms and Microsoft Corporation, which provide the services above, are located outside Turkey. Application data is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

Retention

Application details are kept for the duration of our conversation and any collaboration, and as long as legal obligations require; they are deleted on request. Analytics data is kept for each service's own retention period.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK you can ask whether your data is processed, request information, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, learn who it was shared with, and object to processing. Send your request to @yusufaslancom on Instagram.
